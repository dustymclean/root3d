Frequently asked questions

Is Root3D Consulting the same as other businesses named Rooted Consulting?

Our official name is Root3D Consulting, and our website is r3dconsulting.com. Other businesses using the words Rooted Consulting are separate companies; verify the web address and service focus before booking.

Can Root3D solve issues across cultivation, compliance, retail, and technology?

Yes. The Root3D team is organized around multiple disciplines and can scope cross-functional operational problems, while referring work requiring a licensed professional to an appropriate provider.

Does a discovery call guarantee that Root3D will accept the engagement?

No. The discovery conversation determines fit and scope. A written proposal or quote is needed for paid project work.
