How we work

A practical process that creates usable deliverables

1. Define the problem

We ask for the observed symptoms, decisions at risk, and operational constraints. We agree on the scope before technical work starts.

2. Review the evidence

Depending on the assignment, that may include operating logs, facility diagrams, lab reports, SOPs, inventory records, and site photos.

3. Prioritize actions

The output can include a written findings report, risk-ranked work list, SOP revisions, configuration recommendations, or a scoped implementation plan.

4. Validate what changes

The operator owns decisions and execution; follow-up checks and documentation verify what improved and what still needs attention.

Questions we hear

Frequently asked questions

Can Root3D help us prepare for a Mississippi medical cannabis inspection?

Root3D offers scoped facility and operations reviews, findings documentation, and remediation plans. An inspection result cannot be guaranteed; regulatory authorities make that determination.

Can you troubleshoot recurring cultivation or contamination problems?

The team can review environmental conditions, inputs, irrigation, plant health practices, sanitation workflows, and available testing evidence to identify reasonable next diagnostic steps.

Does Root3D provide legal advice or guarantee compliance?

No. The team provides research, technical assessment, and operational documentation. Legal interpretation and regulated professional services must come from qualified professionals.

Can we start remotely before scheduling a site visit?

Depending on scope, discovery and record review can begin remotely. On-site work and pricing are defined separately when needed.
