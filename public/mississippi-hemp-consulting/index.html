Questions we hear

Frequently asked questions

Does Root3D issue hemp licenses or approve products?

No. Only the appropriate authorities can issue required authorizations, and legal product status requires analysis of the applicable laws, product characteristics, and jurisdiction.

Can you help interpret hemp lab reports?

Root3D can help organize lab data, flag documentation gaps, and explain technical concepts. An accredited lab, regulator, or other qualified professional must be used when required.

Do you consult on greenhouse or controlled-environment hemp projects?

The team can scope facility needs, irrigation and environmental monitoring, workflow design, and records for controlled-environment agricultural projects.

Can you help with hemp supply-chain technology?

Yes. Technology and operations work can include inventory workflows, product data, supplier documentation, security practices, and business system integrations.
